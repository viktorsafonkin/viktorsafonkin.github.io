02 — Artist

“Modern society is turning into a distorting mirror in which Man disappears, is pushed out of space… turning into a biomass capable only of consuming”

Viktor Safonkin was born on 22 August 1967 in Saransk, Mordovia. He received a technical education and is a self-taught painter. Since 1996 he has lived and worked in Prague.

His paintings are about the megalopolis, the underground and the crowd in which a person remains cosmically alone. His figures stand at the centre of the canvas, yet they do not belong to themselves: they are already half objects, half mechanisms, half someone else's dreams. Hence a red dress against a dismantled car, sundresses against a blaze, a smile beneath an iron mask.

His technique is oil: dense brushwork, a muted ground and a single patch of colour torn out of it. Safonkin unites the tradition of Russian figurative painting with the language of Surrealism, which has brought his work into the collections of museums of fantastic realism across Europe.

05 — Book

Teidan

A science-fiction novel by Vladimir Yakovlev about the future. After a catastrophe that has changed the world, its hero Teidan finds himself in an insane reality woven from other people's nightmares, and must fight not only their fears but his own.
